The Challenge

The client needed a landing page that immediately established trust while explaining a complex financial product in simple terms. The previous site suffered from a high bounce rate due to slow loading speeds and a confusing mobile experience.

The Solution

I architected a new frontend from the ground up using React and TailwindCSS. By implementing lazy loading and optimizing the critical rendering path, the initial page load time was reduced by 60%.

To explain the product, I integrated smooth, scroll-linked animations using Framer Motion that visually demonstrated the data flow, making the abstract financial concepts tangible and easy to understand.

The Results

Post-launch analytics showed a 24% increase in the conversion rate for demo sign-ups, and the mobile bounce rate dropped significantly. The clean, modern aesthetic helped position the startup as a premium, reliable service in the fintech space.
