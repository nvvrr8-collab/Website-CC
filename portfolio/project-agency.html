Pushing the Boundaries of the Web

Studio Vision required a portfolio that didn't just display their work, but was a piece of art in itself. The goal was to create an immersive, fluid experience that felt more like a native application than a traditional website.

I utilized Three.js and custom GLSL shaders to build an interactive, fluid-like distortion effect on the hero typography that responds to cursor movement. The page transitions were seamlessly orchestrated using GSAP (GreenSock Animation Platform), ensuring that users never experience a harsh page reload.

Despite the heavy graphics, performance was paramount. Through aggressive asset optimization, texture compression, and dynamic imports, the site maintains a perfect 60fps on both desktop and mobile devices.
